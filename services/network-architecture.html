The infrastructure challenge

Network reliability depends on the whole system.

Security, connectivity, cloud services, and performance must be planned together to support everyday operations.

01

Limited visibility

Without a clear view of the network, risks and performance issues can be difficult to identify.

02

Single points of failure

Critical services need suitable redundancy and recovery planning.

03

Security gaps

Access, segmentation, and firewall controls need to reflect operational requirements.

04

Changing requirements

Infrastructure should adapt as locations, cloud services, users, and workloads change.

The UMES solution

Network architecture designed around your operations.

UMES approaches network architecture as a system of interconnected dependencies. Multi-site topologies, SD-WAN, redundant uplinks, zero-trust controls, cloud routing, and infrastructure diagnostics are designed around the operational requirements of the organization.

We assess the current environment, define a practical target design, and plan changes in a way that supports continuity, security, and future growth.
