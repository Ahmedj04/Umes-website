What we deliver

Built as a system, not a standalone task.

UMES approaches network architecture as a system of interconnected dependencies. Multi-site topologies, SD-WAN, redundant uplinks, zero-trust controls, cloud routing, and infrastructure diagnostics are designed around the operational requirements of the organization.

  • ✓Topology & Backbone Design — End-to-end multi-site schemas, SD-WAN topologies, redundant uplinks, failover pipelines, and low-latency interconnects.
  • ✓Zero-Trust & Security Posture — Enterprise perimeter defenses, NGFW hygiene, micro-segmentation, VPN/VPC architectures, and role-based access control.
  • ✓Cloud & Hybrid Infrastructure — Integration across AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, and on-premises datacenters with load balancing, DNS routing, and disaster recovery architectures.
  • ✓Network Audits & Optimization — Latency profiling, packet bottleneck diagnosis, QoS policies, security assessments, and comprehensive architecture blueprints.
