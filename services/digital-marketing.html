What we deliver

A coordinated marketing service.

UMES combines search engine optimization, Google Ads, Meta Ads, social media marketing, Google Business Profile management, and page promotion into a coordinated marketing service. Campaign decisions are connected to landing pages, tracking, and business goals.

  • ✓Search Engine Optimization — Technical site audits, on-page improvements, local and international SEO, backlink reviews, and structured data to improve organic visibility.
  • ✓Google & Meta Ads — Google Search, Display, and Performance Max campaigns alongside Facebook and Instagram advertising.
  • ✓Performance Marketing — A/B testing, bid strategies, audience segmentation, and performance reporting focused on efficient marketing spend.
  • ✓Social Media Marketing — Profile development, audience engagement, posting schedules, and clear brand communication across LinkedIn, Instagram, and X.
  • ✓Facebook & Instagram Promotion — Targeted post promotions and page campaigns to extend reach and encourage customer enquiries.
  • ✓Google Business Profile Management — Setup, optimisation, profile updates, review guidance, and local search improvements for Google Search and Maps.
