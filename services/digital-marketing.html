What we deliver

Built as a system, not a standalone task.

UMES combines search engine optimization, Google Ads, Meta Ads, performance marketing, social media marketing, and page boosting into a coordinated acquisition system. Campaign decisions are tied back to landing pages, tracking, and business outcomes.

  • ✓Search Engine Optimization — Technical site audits, on-page keyword architecture, local Kashmir/US/UK SEO, backlink profiling, and semantic schema to improve organic visibility.
  • ✓Google & Meta Ads — Targeted Google Search, Display, and Performance Max campaigns alongside Facebook and Instagram ad funnels.
  • ✓Performance Marketing — A/B creative testing, automated bid strategies, audience segmentation, and CAC/LTV calibration geared toward profitable returns.
  • ✓Social Media Marketing — Strategic profile development, audience engagement routines, posting schedules, and unified brand storytelling across LinkedIn, Instagram, and X.
  • ✓FB & IG Page Boosting — Targeted post promotions and follower acceleration to expand reach and drive direct customer inquiries.
