Who we are

One partner for a connected digital business.

Founded in 2025 in Kashmir, Ubiquitous Maintenance & Engineering Services (UMES) is a digital services company bringing together website development, digital marketing, content and analytics, and network architecture.

We believe these services work best when they share a clear purpose. A website should help people understand your business. Marketing should bring relevant audiences to it. Analytics should show what is working, while dependable infrastructure supports the experience behind it all.

That thinking shapes how we work: a coordinated plan, practical technical decisions, and clear communication from the first conversation through launch and ongoing improvement.

Our mission

Make digital progress easier to plan, build, and sustain.

We connect technical delivery with business goals, helping clients choose the services they need and keep improving as their requirements change.
