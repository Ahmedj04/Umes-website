Our approach

Engineering as a discipline, not an afterthought.

UMES began in Kashmir on a simple idea: an online store, an ad funnel, and server infrastructure each hold together only when built with the same rigor as physical architecture.

The typical approach

  • Dev agency, ad agency, and hosting provider operate disconnected
  • Ad campaigns burn budget pointing to slow, unoptimized landing pages
  • Zero conversion tracking or broken Google Analytics setups
  • Support disappears as soon as invoices are settled

The UMES way

  • One synchronized system — website, hosting, ads, and analytics work in unison
  • High-converting builds, engineered specifically to convert paid & organic traffic
  • Complete data fidelity with rigorous Google Analytics (GA4) attribution
  • Ongoing technical support and proactive continuous maintenance
