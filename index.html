Origin

Built on engineering as a discipline

UMES originated in Kashmir as an emerging startup, built on the idea that digital presence — a site, a campaign, a network — holds together only when it's engineered with the same rigor as physical infrastructure.

That principle now shapes how UMES builds: websites, digital marketing, and high-level network architecture, delivered as one coordinated practice with partners across the United States and the United Kingdom.
